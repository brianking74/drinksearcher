# Product

## Register

marketplace

## Users

Hong Kong consumers looking for verified local drink availability, honest pricing, and direct access to suppliers and venues. Also suppliers (merchants, importers, distributors) and venue owners (bars, restaurants, hotels) who want to be discovered by relevant local customers.

## Product Purpose

DrinkSearcher.HK helps Hong Kong drinkers compare bottles in stock across the city, discover bars and events worth their time, and move directly to a supplier or venue — without phantom inventory or misleading overseas pricing. Success is measured by completed discovery-to-action journeys: a consumer who finds a bottle and buys it, or books a table and shows up.

## Brand Personality

Refined, local, editorial, luxurious but practical. DrinkSearcher speaks with the confidence of a Hong Kong insider: it knows the best places, the real prices, and what's actually in stock. The tone is **curated** (not everything is listed, only what's worth knowing), **direct** (here's the price, here's where to buy it), and **local** (Hong Kong districts, MTR stops, WhatsApp). It feels like a premium concierge who also runs a marketplace.

Three-word personality: **curated, local, decisive**.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS marketplaces: flat design, blue CTAs, stock photography of happy people drinking
- Overseas-focused directories: USD pricing, international shipping focus, no local context
- AI-generated "premium" templates: purple gradients, Inter font, glassmorphism, generic wine-bottle silhouettes
- Over-designed hospitality sites that hide the practical information (price, stock, directions)

## Design Principles

1. **Local first.** Hong Kong districts, MTR context, HKD pricing, WhatsApp actions. Every feature answers "can I actually do this from Hong Kong today?"
2. **Trust through transparency.** Show stock freshness, verification status, real prices. No phantom inventory.
3. **Decision engine, not directory.** Every page should help the user decide faster: compare sellers, check stock, book a table.
4. **Editorial luxury.** Dark refined base, champagne/gold accents, rich photography. Feels like a premium guide, not a yellow pages.
5. **Conversion-oriented.** Clear CTAs, save-to-shortlist, direct-to-supplier paths. Beauty serves utility.
6. **Restrained elegance.** Every element earns its place. Gold is the only accent — no competing colors, no decorative flourishes without purpose.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA on all interactive elements
- High-contrast text on dark backgrounds (gold on black meets contrast requirements)
- All interactive elements keyboard-navigable
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected
